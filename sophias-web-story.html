Chester’s Treehouse

A Story In Letter Form · By Sophia

RecommendationYou should do this in a quiet corner.

This is Chester’s treehouse and story:

  1. It is made of wood, and on the door there are brass door hinges.
  2. Inside, there are acorns and nuts and pictures of nuts and acorns.
  3. There is a bridge from the living room door to his friend, Shelly, to her kitchen.
  4. Sometimes he eats his (and hers too) favorite food, raw apples. They are found on her table in the bowl of fruit. But he TAKES (which is stealing) them. But in exchange, he leaves 5 acorns in her bowl of fruit.
  5. Shelly ADORES his house. She loves the red, soft carpet nailed into the floor.
  6. In case Chester wants to sleep in there with Shelly, there are 2 sleeping bags on the floor.
  7. Listen, guys and girls! Chester has a big treehouse. Shelly has a small one.
  8. Chester and Shelly are squirrels.
  9. Flash forward. Shelly and Chester are married in Chester’s Treehouse. They wonder whose treehouse to live in.
  10. They decided on Chester’s treehouse.
  11. Shelly can sew, so she sews four big curtains for the two windows.
  12. About a year later, Shelly has twins, a baby girl named Helene and a boy named Jonnie Boy.
  13. Again a year later. Shelly has a girl named Lena.
  14. The whole family is a squirrel family.
  15. A few years later, Shelly teaches Lena how to sew. Lena learns.
  16. At age 5, Lena is sewing everything, and Helene is just learning how to sew. Lena is gifted. Helene is 6.
  17. Chester decides to show Jonnie Boy how to hammer in nails, put in batteries, etc.
  18. Jonnie Boy learns easily and quickly.
  19. You all know that Chester can run really fast?
  20. Chester teaches Jonnie Boy how to run fast.
  21. At school Jonnie Boy is in 1st grade.
  22. At school Jonnie Boy is the fastest runner.
  23. For her birthday, Lena gets 3 acorns, a sewing kit, and a doll with doll accessories.
  24. At school Lena brings in her sewing kit. Lena’s friend, Goosie, purposely dropped her sewing kit. Small glass pieces were EVERYWHERE. Lena cried and cried for such a long time that Shelly phoned Goosie’s mother, and Goosie came on the phone and Lena came on the phone, and then Goosie said she was sorry for dropping the sewing kit. Then she hung up.
  25. One day, Jonnie Boy just got the hang of it, so at school he just boxed someone. His daddy, Chester, taught him. The principal, Mrs. Melanie, called Shelly.
  26. From now on, I’m gonna call Shelly “Momma” and Chester “Daddy.”
  27. Back to what I was saying. So Mrs. Melanie tried to call Momma, but Daddy answered. Mrs. Melanie said, “Hello, Shelly? Hmm?” Daddy said, “Hello Mrs. Melanie! What’s up, huh?” Mrs. Melanie was shocked from her head to her feet. But anyways she asked if this was Chester or Shelly. Chester answered that it was Chester. Then Mrs. Melanie told about his son and how he boxed somebody on the playground. Chester knew then and there that Jonnie Boy got the hang of it.
  28. After a few years, Momma, Daddy, Jonnie Boy, Helene, and Lena moved to Acorn Valley, where the best nuts were (and raw apples).
  29. In Acorn Valley, they realized they were wrong. They tried the nuts, but they were no good. They tried the apples, but they were no good. So they moved back to Oak Hollow, where Chester’s treehouse was.

P.S. Once Chester made his treehouse, he hardly ever saw his parents.

P.S. again. Chester and his family did very Godly things. They hoped their children would grow up to do this.
