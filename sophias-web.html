Local Reporter Excels At Math, Making Things

Sophia, also learning to bike and drafting her first stories this year

By Sophia · Staff Reporter

Sophia is heading into a new school year, and by all accounts she is ready for it. Teachers and classmates point first to her math skills — she moves through numbers the way other reporters move through a deadline — and second to her knack for making things, whether that means building, crafting with her KiwiCo kits, or dreaming up something new from scratch.

Off the page, she is currently learning to ride a bike, a project she is tackling with the same patience she brings to a hard math problem. She has also taken up writing stories, a hobby this reporter is uniquely qualified to cover.

Summer Travel Desk: Field Trips Top The Itinerary, Again

By Sophia · Travel Correspondent

This reporter has racked up a whole lot of cavities on the record, dental complaints notwithstanding — travel remains the favorite pastime, especially field trips, which rank above ordinary trips in every category that matters. Summers at the family household are consistently described as great, and the family "almost always" goes somewhere when the season arrives.

Editor's note: the claim that the family "almost always" goes somewhere each summer is, by the reporter's own account, based on memory rather than a checked itinerary. This paper stands by her recollection.
